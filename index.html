Materials and finish

Choose by construction, not by marketing labels.

The material, heat treatment, blade geometry and intended use must be considered together. Range names such as Standard, Jangi and Ustaad describe the commission level; they do not identify the steel.

Traditional spring-steel option

A practical option commonly used for straightforward builds. The available alloy, origin and heat-treatment details are confirmed before the order is accepted.

Known high-carbon steel

Heat-treatable blade steel selected for the agreed geometry and intended use. The exact grade is stated in the final specification.

9260 silicon spring steel

A resilient spring steel suited to selected function-led builds. Performance depends on heat treatment, geometry and workmanship rather than the alloy name alone.

Sarabloh

Traditional iron-based construction used for selected smaller shastars. Surface variation and a less uniform finish are part of the material character.

Pattern-welded and specialist steels

Pattern, composition and construction vary. These options are reviewed individually and are not treated as automatic upgrades for every use.

Decorative work

Engraving, silver koftgari, gold koftgari and Ganga-Jamuna are distinct processes. The technique, placement and coverage are recorded separately.

Edge selection: unsharpened, display edge, working sharp and fine cutting edge describe different finishes. The final edge is approved only after the material, geometry, intended use and destination have been reviewed.

Changes after confirmation

Changes may affect the price and timeframe. Once production has reached a stage that cannot be reversed, some changes may no longer be possible.

Handmade variation

Reference images establish the agreed form and finish. Small handmade variations can remain, but important dimensions and defining details are recorded in the specification.

International enquiries

Shipping depends on the destination, courier restrictions and applicable import or possession requirements. Availability is confirmed before payment is accepted.

Custom work

Custom commissions are not treated as off-the-shelf purchases. Deposit, cancellation, alteration and return terms are confirmed with the quotation.

About Akaali Arsenal

Shastar commissions with clearer specifications.

Akaali Arsenal is based in Aotearoa New Zealand and develops commissions through New Zealand-made work, Punjab-made work, or a combination of both. The production route is stated for each order.

What Akaali does

Akaali helps customers define the blade form, proportions, material, fittings and finish before work begins. The aim is to reduce ambiguity and make the final specification easier to assess.

How work is described

Materials and construction are stated directly. Terms such as hand forged, Sarabloh, high-carbon steel, 9260, engraving and koftgari are used only where they apply to the specific piece.

New Zealand and Punjab

Some commissions are made in New Zealand, some are made in Punjab, and some involve work across both. The quotation records the origin and production route rather than applying one claim to the entire range.

Direct communication

Enquiries are reviewed through WhatsApp so references, dimensions and practical requirements can be discussed before the commission is accepted.
